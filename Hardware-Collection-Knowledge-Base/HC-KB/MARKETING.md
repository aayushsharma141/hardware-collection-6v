## Content Pillars (from prior social strategy audit)
1. **Education over selling** — "Manual vs digital locks", "why soft-close matters" (addresses the "Lack of Educational Content" weakness identified earlier)
2. **Behind-the-scenes** — warehouse, team, consultation process (addresses "impersonal brand" weakness)
3. **Sensory/ASMR-style** — sound of soft-close drawers, click of a lock (high-impact, low-cost video format)
4. **Local project spotlights** — real Jamshedpur homes/kitchens, tag local architects
5. **Designer spotlights** — feature a local interior designer weekly, borrow their audience
