# 07 — Sales Playbook

## Greeting
Warm, quick, not pushy. Establish authorized-dealer trust early — this is the single biggest differentiator vs. Sri Ram/Jalaram-type competitors.

## Discovery Questions
- "What are you working on — full kitchen, just handles/locks, or a bigger renovation?"
- "Is this a new build or a renovation?"
- "Any specific brand in mind, or would you like a recommendation?"
- "What's driving the upgrade — security, looks, or something's broken?" *(reveals emotional driver: security/status/durability)*

## Budget Qualification
Ask indirectly: *"Are you looking at our premium German range (Hafele/Hettich) or a more value-focused option (Godrej/Kich)?"* — lets the customer self-select without an awkward direct "what's your budget."

## Brand Recommendation Logic
| Customer signal | Recommend |
|---|---|
| Wants status/premium feel | Hafele, Dorset |
| Security-focused, wants tech | Dorset digital locks |
| Value-conscious, still wants trust | Godrej |
| Kitchen-specific luxury finish | Labacha |

## Cross-Sell / Upsell (from prior revenue-model analysis)
- Door lock purchase → offer matching handle set, door closer, or digital upgrade
- Kitchen sink purchase → offer premium faucet, cabinet organizer
- Any modular kitchen sale → offer soft-close hinge upgrade, drawer organizer add-on

## Quotation SOP
1. Confirm brand + model + quantity.
2. Owner/senior staff confirms current price (see Pricing Rules — never quote from memory on high-ticket items).
3. Share quote via WhatsApp in writing, not verbal only.

## Follow-Up SOP
- Same day: confirm quote received.
- Day 3: soft follow-up if no response — *"Sir, koi aur jaankari chahiye is regarding?"*
- Day 7: final follow-up before marking lead cold in CRM.

| Objection | Response angle |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive vs. local brand" | Reframe on durability + warranty + Jamshedpur humidity resistance, not just brand name |
| "Will it fit my kitchen?" | Offer free on-site/showroom measurement check before commitment |

## Closing Scripts
- "Should I go ahead and block this for you, or would you like to see it installed at the showroom display first?"
- For hesitant buyers: "I can hold this quote for 3 days if you'd like to think it over."

## After-Sales
Log purchase in CRM → schedule review request (SOP.md) → flag for future cross-sell (e.g., kitchen buyer → wardrobe hardware follow-up in 6–12 months, per Customer Personas lifetime-value notes).
