## 12-Step Blueprint (summary — full detail in Notion ops system)
1. Keyword-optimized business name/description (avoid keyword-stuffing the actual name; optimize description instead)
2. Add secondary categories: Bathroom Supply Store, Door Supplier, Kitchen Hardware Store
3. 700–750 character SEO description with local keywords (Sakchi, Bistupur, Kadma, Jamshedpur)
4. Upload 60–80 structured photos (exterior, interior, products, staff, customer purchases)
5. Upload 10 short videos (15–30 sec: store walkthrough, product demos)
6. Add 50–80 products to GBP Products section — each acts as a mini SEO landing page
7. Review acquisition system — WhatsApp-based ask after every purchase
8. Reply to every review within 24 hours, naturally including keywords
9. Weekly Google Posts (new stock, offers, festival hooks — tie to Marketing.md calendar)
10. Activate Q&A section — seed with owner-answered common questions
11. Local citation building — Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, TradeIndia, Facebook (consistent NAP)
12. Behavioral engagement — encourage genuine search → click → call/directions behavior
