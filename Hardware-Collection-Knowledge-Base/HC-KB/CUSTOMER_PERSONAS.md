# 08 — Customer Persona Database

## Persona 1 — The Renovating Homeowner
- **Age:** 30–55, middle-to-upper-middle class
- **Goal:** Upgrade home to feel "designer," durable in Jamshedpur's humid/industrial climate
- **Problems:** Decision fatigue among too many hardware options; fear of choosing wrong finish/brand
- **Buying objections:** Price vs. local unbranded hardware; compatibility with existing layout
- **Preferred communication:** WhatsApp for quick questions, showroom visit for final decision
- **Language used:** "Is it rust-proof?", "Latest design dikhaiye", "Soft-close hai?"
- **Emotional driver:** Pride of ownership, status signaling to guests/family

## Persona 2 — The Architect / Interior Designer
- **Goal:** Reliable sourcing partner for client projects; needs to trust the dealer's stock and quality
- **Problems:** Needs fast quotes, consistent stock, willingness to work on volume/project pricing
- **Buying objections:** Whether the dealer can support project timelines
- **Preferred communication:** WhatsApp broadcast for new stock, direct relationship with owner
- **Opportunity:** Referral/commission program (see Marketing.md)

## Persona 3 — The Builder / Contractor
- **Goal:** Bulk, just-in-time hardware supply with credit terms
- **Problems:** Needs consistent availability, values a dealer who won't run out of stock mid-project
- **Buying objections:** Credit terms, bulk pricing
- **Opportunity:** Contractor discount card / loyalty program (see Marketing.md)

## Persona 4 — The Security-Focused Buyer
- **Goal:** Peace of mind — protecting family/assets
- **Problems:** Technological complexity of digital locks; needs hand-holding on setup
- **Emotional driver:** Safety/security (the strongest trigger identified in prior brand psychology research)
- **Preferred product:** Dorset/Godrej digital locks

## Persona 5 — The Small-Scale Independent Carpenter
- **Goal:** Reliable partner for smaller, frequent hardware needs
- **Problems:** Needs credit flexibility and quick turnaround, not bulk industrial quantities
- **Note:** Currently an underserved segment per competitor analysis — opportunity area
