# 20 — Analytics & Reports Structure

## KPIs to Track Monthly
| KPI | Source | Baseline (Jul 2026) | 90-Day Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMB profile completeness | Google Business Profile | ~55% | 90%+ |
| Google reviews (count/rating) | GBP | ~55 / 4.2-4.4★ | 200+ |
| Monthly calls from GBP | GBP Insights | ~15 | 80-100 |
| Direction requests | GBP Insights | ~30 | 100-120 |
| Website visitors | Vercel Analytics / GA4 | — (pre-launch) | 300-600/mo |
| WhatsApp inquiries | Manual log / WhatsApp Business | — | 5-10/day |
| Instagram followers | Instagram Insights | 0 (pre-launch) | 2,000+ |
| Catalog PDF downloads | Website analytics | — | Track once live |

## Reporting Cadence
- **Weekly:** GBP posts/reviews check (Agent task)
- **Monthly:** Full KPI review against targets above (Aayush + Mukesh)
- **Quarterly:** Competitor tracking refresh (14_COMPETITORS.md)

## Threshold for "Scale Up" Decisions
Only consider heavier tooling (CRM platform, WhatsApp API/chatbot, paid ad campaigns) if:
- WhatsApp inquiries consistently exceed ~15-20/day (manual handling becomes unsustainable), OR
- Website traffic exceeds ~1,000 visitors/month with clear conversion data justifying further investment

Until then, the lightweight structure in this knowledge base (spreadsheet/Notion CRM, manual SOPs, native WhatsApp Business) is the right-sized approach.
