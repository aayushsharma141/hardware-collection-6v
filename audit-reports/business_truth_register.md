## 2. Prohibited Terminology & Marketing Claims

The following terms and patterns are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from appearing anywhere in the public codebase or CMS without written manufacturer/owner authorization:

1. ❌ **No Fabricated MRPs or Price Ranges:** (`₹...`, `Rs.`, `₹₹₹ Luxury Security`, etc.).
2. ❌ **No Same-Day Dispatch Guarantees:** (`Same-day dispatch available`, `Same-day stock availability`).
3. ❌ **No Unsupported Superlatives:** (`No. 1 in Jamshedpur`, `Best hardware store in Jharkhand`, `100% Guaranteed`).
4. ❌ **No Unverified Operating Milestones:** (`21+ Years`, `25+ Years` without confirmed documentation).
5. ❌ **No E-Commerce Terminology:** (`Add to Cart`, `Buy Now`, `Checkout`, `In Stock (12 units)`).
