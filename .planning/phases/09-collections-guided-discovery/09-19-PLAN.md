    a brand logo wall, a category hero and a gallery strip. Every one was given a pinned aspect ratio
    specifically to hold this gate. If CLS has regressed past 0.10, the cause is almost certainly an
    image surface that lost or never received its ratio — find which one rather than treating the
    number as unexplainable.

    Also check the six migrated category images: research flagged them at 600-750KB originals, and
    09-09 was to compress them. If LCP regressed, verify what is actually being served.
  </action>
  <done>LCP, CLS and INP are measured on both routes with real numbers recorded against the thresholds,

<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: Run the manual-only accessibility verifications</name>
  <action>
    Work through the Manual-Only Verifications table in `09-VALIDATION.md`. These cannot be automated
    and must not be skipped on that basis:

    - `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the rail's smooth easing while scroll-snap positioning STILL
      works. Snapping is positioning, not animation; losing it under reduced motion is a bug.
    - Lenis does not hijack the horizontal rail — confirm `data-lenis-prevent` is doing its job by
      trackpad-scrolling the rail and checking the page does not scroll vertically.
    - Keyboard: Tab through `/collections` and a category page. Every interactive element shows a
      visible focus ring, and tab order is sensible. Nothing is reachable only by pointer.
    - Gold-on-black contrast in the SHIPPED page, not the spec: spot-check the brass-on-black text
      pairs and confirm no filled brass button carries bone/white text (the prohibited 1.76:1 pairing).
    - Brand logos render legibly on near-black ground now that real assets are in Sanity.
    - Touch targets meet the 44px floor on mobile.
    - Every visual card carries a visible title, description and action — imagery is never the sole
      affordance, with the one spec-scoped exception of the alphabetical logo wall, whose cells must
      instead carry a per-cell aria-label.
  </action>
  <done>Every manual verification has been performed with its result recorded, including any that failed.</done>
</task>
