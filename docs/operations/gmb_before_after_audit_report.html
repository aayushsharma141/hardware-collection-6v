Products / Services Catalogue
Critical for Retail / E-commerce GMB profiles
⚠ Critical
For a retail/e-commerce business, the Products section is one of the highest-impact GMB features — it creates a visual storefront directly on Google Search and Maps. Currently this section is largely empty, which means potential customers browsing on mobile are not seeing your catalogue.
Add product categories Upload product images per item Set prices where applicable Link each product to website PDP
Q&A Section
Pre-populated questions, owner answers, keyword usage
▲ Needs work
The Q&A section is almost entirely empty. This is a missed SEO and trust opportunity. Anyone can post questions — and without owner-seeded Q&As, random public answers (often incorrect) fill the gap. For retail, questions like "Do you offer returns?", "Do you ship across India?" should already be answered by you.
Seed 8–12 FAQs as owner Use keywords in answers Monitor & respond to new questions
