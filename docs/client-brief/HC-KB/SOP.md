## Walk-in Customer Handling — Owner task (staff-executed)
1. Greet within 30 seconds of entry.
2. Ask what they're renovating/buying for (kitchen, security, wardrobe, etc.) — see Sales Playbook discovery questions.
3. Show relevant brand section; mention authorized-dealer status early (builds trust fast).
4. Offer WhatsApp catalog share if they want to think it over.
5. Log contact info in CRM if genuine interest (see 15_CRM.md).

## Phone Inquiry — Owner task
1. Answer with: *"Hardware Collection, Sakchi — [Name] बोल रहा हूँ, कैसे मदद करूँ?"*
2. Identify need → quote range if possible → invite to showroom or offer WhatsApp catalog.

## WhatsApp Inquiry — Agent task (template) + Owner task (final quote)
1. Auto-greeting sent (see Automation Docs) within seconds.
2. Ask for photo/measurement of the space if relevant (locks, kitchen).
3. Send relevant category catalog PDF.
4. Owner/staff confirms price and availability — pricing is never auto-quoted by any bot without owner review (see Pricing Rules).

## Website Lead — Agent task
1. Lead arrives via WhatsApp CTA click or contact form (if added later).
2. Log into CRM.
3. Respond within 2 business hours during store hours.

## Google Review Request — Agent task
After a confirmed purchase, staff sends: *"Sir/Ma'am, agar service achhi lagi ho to ek Google review de dijiye — bahut madad milti hai."* with the review link.

## Google Business Profile Management — Agent task
Weekly: 1 post, check/reply to all new reviews, check Q&A. See GBP_STRATEGY.md for the full 90-day plan.
