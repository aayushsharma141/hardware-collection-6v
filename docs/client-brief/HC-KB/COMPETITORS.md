# 14 — Competitor Intelligence Database

## Harsh Plywood & Hardware
- **Position:** "Premium & Exclusive" leader
- **Strength:** High rating (~4.8), 160+ reviews, exclusive dealer status (e.g., Kajaria Plywood), strong showroom experience
- **Price tier:** Premium/High
- **Weakness / gap:** Positioned as "exclusive," which can feel intimidating to a middle-class buyer wanting quality without the "boutique" price
- **Our angle:** Compete on "affordable luxury" — branded quality without the intimidation factor

## Sri Ram Hardware (Baridih, Jugsalai)
- **Position:** "Mass Market Reliability" — affordable, everyday repair/construction needs
- **Strength:** Price competitiveness, broad availability
- **Price tier:** Competitive/Low
- **Weakness / gap:** No premium/branded positioning — doesn't compete for the "designer home" buyer
- **Our angle:** We are not competing on price here — we win on brand authorization + expertise

## Jalaram Hardware Stores (Bistupur)
- **Position:** "Industrial & Comprehensive" — contractor/B2B technical solutions
- **Strength:** Reputation, B2B/technical authority
- **Price tier:** Value-driven, bulk pricing
- **Weakness / gap:** Less focus on retail/homeowner-facing digital presence
- **Our angle:** Own the homeowner + designer segment digitally, where Jalaram is weaker

## Market-Wide Gaps (opportunity for Hardware Collection)
1. **Education gap** — no competitor is teaching customers (installation guides, comparison content); see Marketing.md content pillars
2. **Service-first gap** — no competitor packages consultation + installation + after-sales as a core offer, not an afterthought
3. **Mid-range luxury gap** — Harsh is exclusive/premium, Sri Ram is mass-market; there's room for "branded, high-quality, approachable" positioning
