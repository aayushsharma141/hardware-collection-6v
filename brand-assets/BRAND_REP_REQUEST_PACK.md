# Brand Representative Request Pack

This document outlines the required assets and business documentation Hardware
Collection needs to obtain from brand representatives to complete the
production-ready Brand Asset Library for all 21 authorized brands.

---

## Consolidated Checklist

**A. REQUIRED FOR WEBSITE**
- [ ] Official primary logo (vector SVG preferred, high-resolution PNG acceptable)
- [ ] Approved white/reversed logo (for dark backgrounds)
- [ ] Website-approved hero/lifestyle imagery (1-3 images)
- [ ] Confirmation that Hardware Collection may display the brand on its website

**B. USEFUL BUT OPTIONAL**
- [ ] Website-approved product/family imagery (1-3 images)
- [ ] Official catalogue PDF (publicly accessible version)
- [ ] Product catalogue/product-family list
- [ ] Any approved marketing/media kit
- [ ] Brand usage guidelines

**C. BUSINESS DOCUMENTS**
- [ ] Authorized dealer certificate/document
