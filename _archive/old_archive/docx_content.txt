About Sakchi — The Commercial Heart of Jamshedpur
Sakchi is the oldest neighbourhood and the original settlement nucleus of Jamshedpur, India's first planned industrial city. Selected in December 1907 by Jamsetji Tata as the site for what would become Tata Steel, Sakchi has evolved over 115 years into the primary retail and commercial hub of the entire East Singhbhum district.

Critical Developer Mistakes to Avoid
What has been explicitly ruled out — do not overbuild
What This Website Must NOT Be
DO NOT BUILD
This is not an e-commerce site. No shopping cart. No product database. No complex admin panel. No inventory management system. No booking platform. No VPS hosting. No custom CMS. Building any of these is wasted effort and increased maintenance burden for a small retail business.
WRONG APPROACH
CORRECT APPROACH
E-commerce store with cart and checkout
Static landing page with WhatsApp CTAs per category
Complex multi-page site with blog/CMS
1–3 page static site (index + optional contact/about)
VPS or managed server hosting
Vercel free tier — zero server management
Custom product management admin panel
Google Business Profile product catalogue handles this
Topic cluster SEO with 20+ blog posts
GMB posts handle content; website handles schema SEO
Email contact form with backend
WhatsApp deep links — zero backend, instant response
Heavy animation / JavaScript framework
Performance-first — LCP < 2.5s, mobile load < 3s
Separate CSS/JS files per component
Single-file output — inline Tailwind, no bundle complexity
