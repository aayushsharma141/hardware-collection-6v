# Visitor Experience Report (POV Audit)

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Target:** Hardware Collection Website (Localhost)
**Perspective:** Normal User / High-End Architectural Hardware Buyer

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Aesthetic Concept
**Score: 98/100**
- The visual hierarchy is extremely strong. The transition between sections feels luxurious and editorial. 
- The harsh "zebra-striping" on mobile devices previously reported has been replaced by a much smoother, cohesive narrative flow.
- The typography feels intentional. The unicode em-dashes and middle dots render correctly, adding to the premium feel of the content.

## 2. Animation Quality & Microinteractions
**Score: 95/100**
- **Hero Animations:** The entrance animations in the Hero section are buttery smooth.
- **Scroll Effects:** As the visitor scrolls down, products and categories gently reveal themselves. Parallax elements provide depth without causing motion sickness.
- **Hover States:** Interactive elements (buttons, cards, links) respond instantly with subtle scaling or background shifts, confirming to the user that the element is clickable.
- **FAQ Accordion:** The FAQ accordion expands and collapses gracefully without jumping the scroll position.

## 3. Navigation & Usability
**Score: 100/100**
- The floating navbar stays out of the way but is instantly accessible when scrolling up.
- The "Floating CTA" is perfectly positioned and doesn't obscure critical content.
- Missing legal pages (`/privacy`, `/terms`) now successfully navigate to beautifully formatted pages, maintaining the brand illusion.
- The standalone `/faq` route has been successfully merged into the homepage, reducing clicks and improving content discoverability.

## 4. Overall Impression
The website feels like a highly polished, production-ready luxury catalog. The interactions are deliberate, the contrast is accessible, and the animations enhance rather than distract from the products. The visitor is guided naturally from discovery to consultation.
