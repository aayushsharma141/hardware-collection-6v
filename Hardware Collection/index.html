FAQ

Have Questions?

Do you sell Hafele & Dorset products? +
Yes — we are an officially authorized dealer for both Hafele and Dorset. Every product comes with the full manufacturer warranty and genuine certification. Visit our showroom to see the full range.
Do you offer bulk pricing for builders? +
Absolutely. We work regularly with builders, contractors, and interior designers across Jamshedpur and Adityapur. WhatsApp us your requirement list and we’ll send a project quote within 2 hours.
Do you deliver same day in Jamshedpur? +
Yes — for all in-stock items, we offer same-day delivery across Jamshedpur including Bistupur, Mango, Golmuri, Sonari, and Adityapur. Call or WhatsApp us to confirm stock availability.
Can you help with hardware selection? +
Yes — this is what we do best. Free consultation on choosing the right hardware for your space. Visit the showroom or WhatsApp us a photo of your space and we’ll guide you.
Do you have a digital lock demo at the store? +
Yes — we have a dedicated Dorset digital lock experience zone. Test fingerprint, OTP, and app-based access in person. Walk in anytime between 10AM–8PM.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cash, all major debit & credit cards, UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), and NFC mobile payments. For large project orders, invoice with advance payment options are available.
